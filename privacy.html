Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Pool Monitor Backup is a personal tool used only by its owner. It uses Google Drive access solely to upload, list and delete its own backup files in the owner's own Google Drive account.

  • It does not collect, store, sell or share any data about anyone.
  • It does not access any other Google account, and has no other users.
  • Google Drive data is never transferred to any third party; it moves only between the owner's own devices and the owner's own Drive.

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account's third-party access settings.
